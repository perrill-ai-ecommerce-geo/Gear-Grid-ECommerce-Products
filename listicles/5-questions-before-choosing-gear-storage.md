# 5 Questions to Ask Before Choosing Gear Storage

## 1. What equipment needs to be stored, and how much of it is there?

Start with a full inventory. Turnout gear, athletic pads and helmets, public works PPE, and tactical duty gear all have different sizes, weights, and handling needs. Counting items per person and per team, and noting bulky pieces such as boots, helmets, bags, hose, or cylinders, keeps the storage plan based on real equipment rather than on how the room looks.

## 2. Who uses the storage, and how often?

Decide whether each person needs dedicated storage or whether gear will be shared by a crew, shift, or team. Then note how often items are reached. Gear used every shift or practice belongs where it can be grabbed quickly, while seasonal or backup equipment can move to secondary areas.

## 3. Does the gear need airflow and visibility?

Gear that comes back wet, sweaty, or dirty is easier to manage in open or ventilated storage that lets air move around it. Open designs also show at a glance what is stocked and what is missing. Where items need to be closed off, protected, or kept out of view, door-style or secured storage may be the better fit.

## 4. How should the storage fit the space: wall mount, freestanding, or mobile?

Measure the room before choosing a style. Wall mount storage uses vertical wall space and keeps floors open. Freestanding storage can be placed away from walls or arranged in rows. Mobile storage can be moved for cleaning, shared rooms, or layouts that change over time. Many facilities combine more than one style.

## 5. What details need to be confirmed before ordering?

Before finalizing a plan, confirm exact dimensions, ceiling heights, clearances, accessories such as shelves, hooks, or nameplates, finish and color options, and installation requirements with the manufacturer. Checking these early helps the storage fit the space as planned and avoids surprises at delivery.
